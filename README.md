# Click2Check customer screens

Clickable demo of the customer link screens, on a phone and in a computer's browser:
the Terms & Conditions landing page with a reading progress bar, Confirm Your Details,
the mobile layout and Click2Check styling. Links keep working until the customer finishes.

Made-up customer, no backend: a fake hub inside the page answers every request, so nothing
contacts Click2Check, Equifax or anyone else.

Open: https://click2checkdev.github.io/Customer-screens-demo/
