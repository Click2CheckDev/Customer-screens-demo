# Click2Check customer screens: today and the update

Side-by-side clickable demo of the customer link screens, on a phone and on a computer:
today's live version next to the update (Terms & Conditions landing page with a reading
progress bar, mobile layout, Click2Check styling, links that keep working until the customer
finishes).

Made-up customer, no backend: a fake hub inside the page answers every request, so nothing
contacts Click2Check, Equifax or anyone else.

Open: https://click2checkdev.github.io/Customer-screens-demo/
